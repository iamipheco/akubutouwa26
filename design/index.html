The tradition

What happens at an Igba Nkwu

Igba Nkwu means “wine carrying”. It is the Igbo traditional marriage, and a cup of palm wine sits at the heart of it.

  1. I · Welcome

    Ọjị, the kola nut

    Elders bless and break the kola nut to welcome guests and open the gathering.

  2. II · The cup

    A cup of palm wine

    Chidimma’s family fills a cup with palm wine and places it in her hands.

  3. III · The search

    She finds her husband

    She walks through the crowd looking for Tochukwu while guests call out to distract her. When she finds him, she kneels and offers him the cup. He drinks, and everyone knows her choice.

  4. IV · Celebration

    Dance, music and the feast

    The couple dances to the cheers of family and friends, and the feasting begins.
